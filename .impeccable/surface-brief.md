# Reading and curation

Mode: Read on homepage; Operate on review workbench.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A daily reading desk with real headlines, search and publisher navigation.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper #f7f5ef, ink #252820, olive #52613b, rules #deddd3, serif Chinese reading headings and sans controls. No decorative card grid.

STORY: Readers browse a consistent snapshot, filter by publisher or title, and follow originals. Curators keep decisions locally.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared masthead; editorial heading and snapshot date; publisher index beside a headline column. Search above the list, pagination below. Review has compact filters and visible decision tools.

FORM: User-selected editorial desk, code-led. The explicit user choice pins the world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
