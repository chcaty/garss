# Android RSS reader

Mode: Read for articles; Operate for source and reading preferences.

THESIS: A usable Android reading app consumes the existing immutable Pages snapshots and keeps personal reading state on the device.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit warm paper, olive controls, thin rules, the existing RSS logo and the licensed editorial heading. Use Android native navigation, back behavior, safe areas and accessibility scaling.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Reading heading, live synchronization status, search, unread filter, source selector, then real article titles, summaries and optional publisher images. Reading/favorites/sources/settings remain within thumb reach in bottom navigation.

SIGNATURE: Source rotation gives quieter publishers a turn before repeated posts from high-volume feeds; latest order remains available. Saved article metadata survives the server's retention window.

CONTENT: Publisher content stays plain text in summaries; original pages open in a WebView or external browser. Catalog/summary/favorites are offline; remote images and originals are not guaranteed offline. No user account or cross-device sync is implied.

FINISH: Independent review of native screenshots and behavior; document Android-specific decisions without replacing the web system.
